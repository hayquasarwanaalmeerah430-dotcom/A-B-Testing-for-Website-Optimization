Verdict

✅ Ship Variant B

Variant B lifts conversion by +1.12 pp (95% CI 0.60 to 1.64 pp; z-test p <0.001, chi-square agrees at p <0.001), cuts bounce by 7.7 pp and lengthens sessions — with no guardrail metric moving the wrong way. Projected impact: ≈ +112 extra signups per 10,000 sessions.
